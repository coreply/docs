Coreply works with the following messaging apps on Android. Apps marked with a limitation note may have reduced functionality in specific areas.

| App | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| WhatsApp | Full support |
| Instagram | Full support |
| Tinder | Full support |
| Signal | Full support |
| Hinge | Full support |
| LINE | Full support |
| Heymandi | Full support |
| Gmail | Only works on the quick reply text field at the bottom of the email |
| Telegram | Includes Direct Download version, Play Store version, and Nekogram |
| Mattermost | Limited role detection: Coreply cannot detect whether the message is sent or received |
| Facebook Messenger | Performance issues: Coreply may not follow smoothly the animations and transitions |
| Google Messages | Performance issues: Coreply may not follow smoothly the animations and transitions |
| Snapchat | Limited role detection: Coreply cannot detect whether the message is sent or received |
| Microsoft Teams | Full support |
| Viber | Full support |
| Discord | Full support |
| Beeper | Full support |
| Notification Replies | Performance issues; limited role detection |

## Enabling Coreply for specific apps

You can control which apps Coreply is active in using the **Select Apps** feature in the app settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Coreply">
    Launch the Coreply app. The main screen shows the service controls and settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Select Apps">
    Tap **Select Apps** to open the list of supported apps installed on your device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your apps">
    Toggle on the apps where you want Coreply to provide suggestions. Coreply will only activate in the apps you select.
  </Step>
</Steps>


# Using Coreply with unsupported apps

You can select apps that are not in the supported list. Coreply will try to extract the on screen text and provide suggestions. However, the behavior is not guaranteed. Please do not open issues for unsupported apps, unless you are certain the problem is due to a bug in Coreply.

<Note>
  Coreply cannot read images, videos, voice notes, or other non-text content. Suggestions may be limited in conversations that contain media.
</Note>
